Everyday Smart Habits

Simple tips for better focus, health, and daily life

Quick Daily Wins

  • Morning: Drink 1 glass of water before checking your phone.
  • Work: Take a 5-minute break every hour to rest your eyes.
  • Evening: Dim bright screens 30 minutes before sleep.
  • Anytime: Take 3 slow, deep breaths when feeling stressed.

FOCUS TIP The 2-Minute Rule

Small chores—like washing a plate, replying to a short message, or putting away clothes—often pile up because we keep putting them off.

How to use it: If a task takes less than two minutes to finish, do it right away instead of saving it for later.

HEALTH TIP The 20-20-20 Eye Rule

Staring at phones, TVs, or computers for hours makes your eyes tired and can cause headaches. Giving your eye muscles a quick break keeps your vision comfortable.

How to use it: Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away (like out a window) for at least 20 seconds.
